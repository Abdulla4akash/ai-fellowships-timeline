## ⚡ Executive Summary & Timeline Overview

AI fellowships generally operate across two distinct annual submission windows:

1. **Autumn / Winter Intake (September – January)**:
   - **Primary Focus**: Top-tier tech industry PhD fellowships (Meta, NVIDIA, IBM, Jane Street, Apple), US federal doctoral grants (NSF GRFP, NDSEG, Hertz), UK flagship fellowships (Royal Society URF & Dorothy Hodgkin, UKRI Turing AI Pioneer), and winter AI safety cohorts (ERA:AI Cambridge, MATS Winter).
   - **Characteristics**: Extremely high funding volume, multi-year awards, full tuition + competitive living stipends ($37,000–$60,000+ / £20,000–£35,000+).
2. **Spring Intake (February – May)**:
   - **Primary Focus**: Google PhD Fellowship, European Qualcomm Innovation Fellowship, Leverhulme Trust Early Career Fellowships, GovAI Summer Fellowship, MATS Summer Cohort, Schmidt Science Fellows, and Royal Academy of Engineering (RAEng) Research Fellowships.
   - **Characteristics**: Focuses heavily on institutional nominations, summer research intensives, and transition fellowships for postdocs and early-career investigators.
3. **Rolling / Seasonal Cohorts**:
   - OpenAI Residency, Anthropic Fellows & Interpretability Programs, and UK/US AI Safety Institute opportunities.

> **CRITICAL WARNING ON INTERNAL UNIVERSITY DEADLINES**: Many prestigious fellowships (e.g., Google PhD, Apple Scholars, Schmidt Science Fellows, Turing AI Pioneer, Leverhulme ECF) require **institutional nomination**. Your university or department will enforce an **internal sift deadline that is 4 to 8 weeks earlier** than the national/sponsor deadline listed below!

## 🏆 Key Fellowships by Category

### 1. Frontier Tech & Industry PhD Fellowships

Corporate research fellowships offer some of the highest financial stipends and unrestricted research funds available in AI, accompanied by direct access to industrial GPU compute clusters and mentorship.

#### [Meta Research PhD Fellowship](https://metaresearch.facebook.com/fellowship/)
- **Target Audience**: Full-time PhD candidates anywhere in the world conducting research in machine learning, computer vision, natural language processing, audio, or robotics.
- **Deadline**: **September 20, 2026**
- **Award & Benefits**:
  - 2 full academic years of tuition and fees paid.
  - $42,000 annual living stipend.
  - $5,000 annual conference travel grant.
  - Pairing with a Meta Research mentor and invitation to the annual Fellowship Summit.
- **Application Requirements**: Research summary (1–2 pages), CV, 2 recommendation letters.

#### [Apple Scholars in AI/ML PhD Fellowship](https://machinelearning.apple.com/)
- **Target Audience**: PhD candidates in their final 2–3 years at invited partner institutions.
- **Deadline**: **September 8, 2026** *(Note: Universities enforce internal cutoffs in July/August)*
- **Award & Benefits**: Full tuition and fees, competitive living stipend, mentorship from Apple AI researchers, and internship invitation.
- **Application Requirements**: Institutional nomination only. Check with your department chair or graduate school office.

#### [NVIDIA Graduate Fellowship](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/research/graduate-fellowships/)
- **Target Audience**: PhD students who have completed their first year in AI, GPU computing, robotics, or architecture.
- **Deadline**: **Mid-September** (Annually ~September 15)
- **Award & Benefits**: Up to $60,000 per award, access to NVIDIA GPU clusters, and direct collaboration with NVIDIA Research teams.

#### [IBM PhD Fellowship Award](https://research.ibm.com/university/awards/fellowship)
- **Target Audience**: PhD students in participating universities nominated by department faculty.
- **Deadline**: **Mid-September** (Annually ~September 16 nomination cutoff)
- **Award & Benefits**: $40,000 stipend (USA) or local equivalent (UK/EU) per year, education allowance, and IBM researcher mentor.

#### [Jane Street Graduate Research Fellowship](https://www.janestreet.com/join-jane-street/programs-and-events/graduate-research-fellowship/)
- **Target Audience**: Graduate students (2nd–4th year PhD) in CS, ML, mathematics, and statistics in the US, UK, and globally.
- **Deadline**: **November 13, 2026 (23:59 AoE)**
- **Award & Benefits**:
  - $45,000 unrestricted living stipend.
  - Full tuition and university fees covered for 1 academic year.
  - Zero intellectual property claims; all-expenses-paid research symposium at Jane Street NYC.

#### [Qualcomm Innovation Fellowship (QInF)](https://www.qualcomm.com/research/university-relations/innovation-fellowship)
- **Regions & Deadlines**:
  - **North America**: **December 1, 2026** (~$100,000 per winning pair of PhD students).
  - **Europe & UK**: **January 28, 2027** (€40,000 / £35,000 per award).
- **Format**: Teams of two PhD students submit a collaborative proposal in AI/ML architectures, wireless, edge computing, or autonomous systems.

#### [Microsoft Research Fellowship Program](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/academic-programs/fellowships/)
- **Target Audience**: Doctoral candidates and early-career postdocs collaborating on frontier AI.
- **Deadline**: **December 16, 2026** *(Call opens November 4)*
- **Award & Benefits**: Direct research grants, Azure cloud credits, and joint research projects with Microsoft Research labs worldwide.

#### [Google PhD Fellowship](https://research.google/outreach/phd-fellowship/)
- **Target Audience**: Exemplary PhD students nominated by eligible universities in the US, UK, and worldwide.
- **Deadline**: **April 30, 2027** *(Internal university nominations close February/March)*
- **Award & Benefits**: Up to 3 years full tuition and fees, $40,000–$60,000/year living stipend (country dependent), and 1-on-1 Google Research mentorship.

## 🎯 Strategic Application Playbook

### 1. Navigating Institutional Nominations
Many premier fellowships (Google PhD, Apple Scholars, Schmidt Science, UKRI Turing AI) do **not** accept direct open applications from students.
- **Action Plan**:
  1. Contact your university's Graduate Studies Office / Research Development Office **at least 8 weeks prior** to the sponsor deadline.
  2. Ask for the internal Expression of Interest (EOI) deadline and candidate submission guidelines.
  3. Ensure your department head or academic advisor is prepared to submit an institutional endorsement letter.

### 2. Crafting a Winning AI Research Statement
- **Problem Significance**: Articulate not just technical novelty, but why solving this problem matters to the broader scientific community or society.
- **Feasibility & Resource Plan**: Specify exactly what compute (GPUs/TPUs) and dataset resources you require and how your host institution supports this.
- **Intellectual Autonomy**: Differentiate your personal research direction from your advisor's overarching grant objectives.

### 3. Recommendation Letters That Stand Out
- Provide referees with a comprehensive packet at least 4 weeks in advance: your CV, proposal draft, the fellowship's evaluation rubric, and 3 specific bullet points you would like them to emphasize.

### 4. Visa & Mobility Considerations
- **UK Host Institutions**: Many UK fellowships (Royal Society, Leverhulme, ERA:AI) qualify for fast-track endorsement under the **UK Global Talent Visa** or **Temporary Work – Government Authorised Exchange (GAE)** route.
- **USA Host Institutions**: Most US fellowships for international scholars sponsor **J-1 Research Scholar** or **F-1 OPT/STEM OPT** status. Check with the host International Students and Scholars Office (ISSO) early.

## 💻 CLI Quick Filter

A built-in Python CLI tool is available to query and filter the fellowship dataset directly from your terminal:

```bash
# List all fellowships in the UK
python3 scripts/filter_timeline.py --country UK

# List all fellowships in the USA
python3 scripts/filter_timeline.py --country USA

# Filter by category (e.g. Industry PhD)
python3 scripts/filter_timeline.py --category "Industry PhD"

# Filter by AI Safety & Alignment
python3 scripts/filter_timeline.py --category "Safety"

# Sort chronologically by soonest deadline
python3 scripts/filter_timeline.py --upcoming
```
