## ⚡ Executive Summary & Timeline Overview

AI opportunities generally fall across three formats:

1. **Short-Term Intensive Sprints & Residencies (8–12 Weeks)**:
   - **Key Feature**: Fully funded (stipends + housing/travel) with **substantial dedicated compute budgets** (thousands of dollars in cloud GPU credits, dedicated A100/H100 clusters, or supercomputing access).
   - **Examples**: Pivotal Research Fellowship (London), Frontier Development Lab (NASA/ESA), Cohere For AI Scholars, MATS (Berkeley/London), ERA:AI (Cambridge), SPAR (Berkeley), FAR.AI, and CERN openlab.
   - **Target**: PhD students on summer break, graduate students, ML engineers, and independent safety/alignment researchers.
2. **Multi-Year PhD & Graduate Fellowships (1–5 Years)**:
   - **Key Feature**: Full tuition coverage + living stipends ($37k–$60k / £20k–£35k) + travel allowances.
   - **Examples**: Meta PhD Fellowship, Google PhD Fellowship, NSF GRFP, NDSEG, Hertz, Apple Scholars, NVIDIA Graduate Fellowship, Jane Street GRF.
3. **Postdoctoral & Career Independence Fellowships (1–8 Years)**:
   - **Examples**: Royal Society URF & Dorothy Hodgkin, Schmidt Science Fellows, UKRI Turing AI Pioneer, Leverhulme Trust ECF, Stanford HAI Postdoctoral Fellowship.

## 🏆 Key Fellowships by Category

### 1. 8–12 Week Funded Sprints & Training Residencies

These intensive programs provide focused research mentorship, full living stipends, and massive GPU/TPU compute budgets for rapid prototyping and paper publication.

#### [Pivotal Research Fellowship (AI Safety & Interpretability)](https://pivotalfellowship.org)
- **Host**: Pivotal & London Initiative for Safe AI (LISA)
- **Location**: London, United Kingdom (Open to international applicants)
- **Duration**: **9 weeks full-time**
- **Deadline**: **November 10, 2026** (Spring 2027 intake)
- **Funding & Stipend**: £6,000 – £8,000 living stipend + £2,000 accommodation and travel support + free office space and catered meals at LISA London.
- **Compute Budget**: **£2,500 – £5,000+ dedicated compute credits** on high-end GPU clusters (A100/H100).
- **Mentors**: 1-on-1 pairing with researchers from Google DeepMind, Redwood Research, and leading safety labs.
- **Extension Opportunity**: Approximately 70% of recent fellows receive multi-month fully funded extensions.

#### [Frontier Development Lab (FDL - NASA, ESA & SETI)](https://fdl.ai/)
- **Host**: Trillium Technologies, NASA, European Space Agency (ESA), Google Cloud & NVIDIA
- **Location**: Mountain View, CA (USA) & Oxford (UK) / Hybrid
- **Duration**: **8 weeks full-time** (June – August)
- **Deadline**: **April 10, 2027** (Applications open Jan/Feb 2027)
- **Funding & Stipend**: Fully funded weekly stipend ($1,000–$1,200/week, ~$8,000–$10,000 total) + travel and accommodation.
- **Compute Budget**: **Massive enterprise supercomputing allocations** provided by Google Cloud, NVIDIA, Intel, and AWS ($100,000+ in compute value per team).
- **Focus**: Applying deep learning to planetary defense, heliophysics, lunar exploration, and climate resilience.

#### [Cohere For AI (C4AI) Scholars Program](https://cohere.com/research/scholars)
- **Host**: Cohere For AI
- **Location**: Remote-first (USA, UK, Canada, and worldwide)
- **Duration**: **12 weeks to 6 months**
- **Deadline**: **February 15, 2027**
- **Funding & Stipend**: Fully paid competitive researcher salary (**$56 – $64/hr**, approx. $9,000–$10,000/month).
- **Compute Budget**: **Direct access to Cohere's enterprise multi-thousand GPU/TPU training clusters** for training and probing large language models.
- **Focus**: Foundational NLP, open-source multilingual foundation models (Aya project), multimodal reasoning, and mechanistic interpretability.

#### [Iliad Fellowship in Applied Mathematics for AI Alignment](https://iliadfellowship.org/)
- **Host**: Iliad & PrincInt
- **Location**: London / Oxford, United Kingdom & USA
- **Duration**: **12 weeks (3 months)**
- **Deadline**: **November 25, 2026** (Spring 2027 Cohort)
- **Funding & Stipend**: Fully funded living stipend + private accommodation in London + international travel support.
- **Compute Budget**: Dedicated GPU compute allocations for empirical verification of learning dynamics, singular learning theory, and agent models.

#### [Supervised Program for Alignment Research (SPAR)](https://berkeleyalignment.github.io/spar/)
- **Host**: UC Berkeley AI Alignment
- **Location**: UC Berkeley (USA) & Global / Remote
- **Duration**: **10 to 12 weeks**
- **Deadline**: **January 15, 2027** (Spring Round)
- **Funding & Stipend**: $2,000 – $5,000 mentee research grants based on project commitment.
- **Compute Budget**: Dedicated cloud GPU compute grants via RunPod, Lambda Labs, and Berkeley AI cluster credits.
- **Focus**: Pairs rising talent with experienced mentors from CHAI, Anthropic, Redwood Research, and OpenAI across 130+ safety research topics.

#### [FAR.AI Alignment Research Sprints & Fellowship](https://far.ai/)
- **Host**: FAR AI (Field Alignment Research)
- **Location**: Berkeley, CA, USA
- **Duration**: **8 to 12 weeks full-time**
- **Deadline**: **February 1, 2027** (Summer 2027 Sprint)
- **Funding & Stipend**: **$7,000/month stipend** (~$14,000–$21,000 total) + travel reimbursement and housing support in Berkeley.
- **Compute Budget**: High-priority access to FAR AI's dedicated H100/A100 GPU clusters for red-teaming, jailbreak defense, and automated model auditing.

#### [Apart Lab Fellowship & Research Sprints](https://apartresearch.com/)
- **Host**: Apart Research
- **Location**: Remote / London & San Francisco hubs
- **Duration**: **8 to 12 weeks**
- **Deadline**: **October 31, 2026** (Winter Sprint)
- **Funding & Stipend**: $4,000 – $8,000 research project stipend.

- **Host**: CERN (European Organization for Nuclear Research)

#### [EleutherAI Resident & Research Fellowship](https://www.eleuther.ai/)
- **Host**: EleutherAI Institute

## 🎯 Strategic Application Playbook

### 1. How to Secure Large Compute Budgets in Applications
- **Quantify Hardware Needs**: Estimate total FLOPs or GPU-hours realistically (e.g. *"We require 1,200 A100-hours for pre-training probes and 400 H100-hours for fine-tuning validation"*).
- **Emphasize Efficiency**: Highlight efficient ML techniques you use (LoRA, QLoRA, FlashAttention, quantization) to show reviewers you are a responsible steward of compute.
- **Compute Matching**: Mention existing cluster or API access (e.g., RunPod credits, campus clusters) that will amplify the fellowship's funding.

### 2. Navigating University Sifts & Institutional Nominations
- Many programs (Google PhD, Apple Scholars, Schmidt Science, UKRI Turing AI) enforce strict quotas (e.g., 2–4 applicants per university).
- Inquire with your department head or graduate research office **8 weeks ahead** to ensure your dossier passes internal shortlisting.

## 💻 CLI Quick Filter

A built-in Python CLI tool is available to query and filter the dataset directly from your terminal:

```bash
# Filter specifically for 8-12 week funded programs with compute budgets
python3 scripts/filter_timeline.py --category "8-12"

# List all fellowships and training programs in the UK
python3 scripts/filter_timeline.py --country UK

# List all programs in the USA
python3 scripts/filter_timeline.py --country USA

# Filter by Industry PhD Fellowships
python3 scripts/filter_timeline.py --category "Industry PhD"

# Sort chronologically by soonest deadline
python3 scripts/filter_timeline.py --upcoming
```
